## Row-by-row claim judgment

1. `a6e5226259c9` — system-pressure-r24.md `kernel/sched/psi.c:845-857` (U7). Claim: writing 0 to the root
   `cgroup.pressure` stops `psi_system` accounting; `psi_group_change` stops recording. v7.3 lines 845-857 are
   the `if (!group->enabled)` branch inside `psi_group_change` (function defined at v7.3 psi.c:796): it flushes
   the live state once (`record_times` under PSI_NONIDLE), sets `groupc->state_mask`, and `return`s before the
   normal `record_times`/state tracking at 875-881. Ongoing recording stops; totals freeze. Supported at v7.3.
2. `0124918d278a` — system-pressure-r24.md `kernel/sched/psi.c:551,1269-1270` (U7). Claim: totals freeze and the
   averages decay toward zero over the following reads. psi.c:551 is the delta sample inside `update_averages`'s
   per-state loop (`sample = group->total[PSI_AVGS][s] - group->avg_total[s];`); with recording stopped the sample
   is 0 each period and the exponential-decay loop drives the averages toward zero. psi.c:1269-1270 is the
   `psi_show` read path: `if (now >= group->avg_next_update) group->avg_next_update = update_averages(group, now);`
   — averages are refreshed on read when the 2-second update is due. Both halves supported at v7.3.
3. `6f9f1d32ac46` — system-pressure-r24.md `kernel/sched/psi.c:1538-1550` (round-27 corrections log). The entry
   cites this range only in the correction record (entry line 241): "`psi.c:1538-1550` … → `psi.c:1540,1545,1550,1686`:
   the four `psi_show(m, &psi_system, …)` call sites; 1538-1550 holds two full readers and a signature, not four
   readers." v7.3 lines 1538-1550 (identical to v7.2) contain `psi_io_show` (1538-1541), `psi_memory_show`
   (1543-1546) and `psi_cpu_show` truncated at 1550 (closing brace at 1551) — i.e. two full readers plus a third
   partial, not four. The four call sites named by the correction are confirmed at v7.3: 1540 (PSI_IO), 1545
   (PSI_MEM), 1550 (PSI_CPU), 1686 (`psi_irq_show`, PSI_IRQ under CONFIG_IRQ_TIME_ACCOUNTING). The correction
   statement is accurate at v7.3.
