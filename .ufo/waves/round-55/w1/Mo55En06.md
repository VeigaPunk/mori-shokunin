are in block-size units. Entailed.

Row 11 / 4aab9fc1a48b (S537, xfs_super.c.v7.3:950-954) — `xfs_statfs_inodes()`:

bits; else buf->f_bfree = 0;`. Reserve subtracted from f_bfree; clamp-to-zero only deepens the
shortfall, so "sits below raw unallocated capacity by that reserve" holds (exactly by the reserve
