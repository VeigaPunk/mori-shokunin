# C1 metric adversarial review

## Verdict

**The 116→0 result is useful as a narrowly scoped, frozen regex-hit metric, but it is not a privacy-clearance result.** The fixed `must_redact_paths` rule prevents allowlisting the three named reports, yet allowlists, regex recall, non-author exclusions, and Git history remain material limits. Keep the frozen policy and metric intact; require an independent content review and state the boundaries whenever reporting a pass.

## Counterexample and evidence

- **The routed 40 is not a count of the spot-check's cited line ranges.** The frozen population records 40 class-hit rows in the three required-to-redact files: OmpRetryConfig has 17 hits / 16 distinct lines, PacmanActorTrace 18 / 17, and SessionExitCorrelation 5 / 4. Grouping the same records against the exact ranges in the spot-check yields 23 hit rows (12 + 10 + 1); the other 17 hits occupy 14 lines outside those ranges. Thus 40 is the policy's broader *whole-file regex-match count*, not a faithful numeric encoding of the spot-check's routed ranges. It is defensible—and safer—as a whole-file target because the policy names whole files, but the report must not imply that 40 equals the source ranges' contents. Sources: `corpus/lessons/round-17-verifier-spotcheck.md:339-346`; `.ufo/waves/round-18/frozen/c1-policy.json:17-22,28`; `.ufo/waves/round-18/frozen/c1-population.json:97-197,223-327,415-443`.
- **40 counts class matches, not unique lines or disclosure units.** The population summary is 119 total hit rows, 3 non-author rows, and metric-before 116 (`.ufo/waves/round-18/frozen/c1-population.json:5-10`). In the three routed files, 40 hit rows collapse to 37 distinct lines because three lines match two classes each: population records for OmpRetryConfig line 4, PacmanActorTrace line 66, and SessionExitCorrelation line 12 (`.ufo/waves/round-18/frozen/c1-population.json:97-107,272-281,422-430`). That is not a defect if the unit is clearly called a class-hit count; it is misleading if 116 or 40 is presented as a count of unique retained passages.
- **Allowlisting can create a false green outside the three protected paths.** The metric is explicitly unallowlisted hits, and the policy permits rules a/b/c for qualifying rows; the gate cannot establish from a row's claimed reason alone that a matching line contains no copied private value. The explicit no-allowlist rule for `must_redact_paths` blocks this shortcut for the 40 routed hits, but the 116 baseline also includes other files. Require a human review of every active allowlist row against its line and source; an unsubstantiated or false rule assignment is a C1 failure even if the count reaches zero. A stale-row warning is not a substitute for that review. Sources: `.ufo/waves/round-18/frozen/c1-policy.json:17-27`; `contract.md:55-61`.
- **A paraphrase can evade the regex count.** The policy itself says its classes are a recall floor and private context without the tokens is found only by a separate reading sweep. It also skips NUL-containing binary blobs and decodes text with UTF-8 replacement. Therefore a zero scan can coexist with retained paraphrased, encoded, or otherwise unrecognized content. The contract's broader minimization requirement must be evidenced by an independent whole-tree review, not inferred from the scanner. Sources: `.ufo/waves/round-18/frozen/c1-policy.json:5-15,27-29`; `contract.md:9-13`.
- **The non-author exclusion is observable, not hypothetical.** Three excluded hit rows remain in `corpus/lessons/round-17-verifier-spotcheck.md`: line 312 (R9, SHA-256 `b01e7483a8095ced090ce2b6400948474fc570c0c3e9b289338bb2dfacafb1d2`), line 315 (R6, `a05f9cb73a366c5fa59a9cf66b78d75a489b14fd9e2f2c2b1018d67088957ffe`), and line 341 (R6, `25ea86d2f05b5505bcc34420af7ca70558b415d4b47417c3e14cace80c7433fe`). The 116 metric excludes them. The policy's broad round-wildcard exclusion also makes path matching alone insufficient proof that a future matching file is non-author-owned. Keep these residuals explicitly routed and verify ownership; do not call the tree retention-free. Sources: `.ufo/waves/round-18/frozen/c1-policy.json:22-28`; `.ufo/waves/round-18/frozen/c1-population.json:703-719`.
- **Git history remains an explicit non-goal.** The contract says history is not rewritten and assigns exposure assessment to the operator/Kagi. A clean scan at the measured revision cannot erase prior commits. This is an accepted boundary for the scoped gate, but it falsifies any broader statement that prior disclosure or public exposure has been removed. Source: `contract.md:11-13,24-29`.

## Axes impacted

- **Adjacency — wrong axis for the 116→0 deletion metric.** `AXES.md:16` defines adjacency as reporting evidence belonging to another domain, not absorbing it. Removing private-context copies from lane-owned files is not itself that cross-domain behavior. The explicit handoff of excluded non-author and historical exposure is adjacency work; the regex-hit reduction does not measure it (and excludes the non-author rows). Keep adjacency only for the separately evidenced handoff.
- **Coverage — the closest measurable axis for scan completeness, with conditions.** `AXES.md:12` defines coverage by the real surfaces governed. A whole-tracked-tree scan can support coverage only when the separate reading sweep covers paraphrases and the report preserves binary, allowlist, and ownership exclusions. A regex-only zero is not enough.
- **Calibration — required for the pass wording.** Report the result as zero unallowlisted class hits in the scanned tracked-text snapshot, not zero private material or zero exposure. The history/non-author caveats are known, not resolved by the count.

## Strongest post-integration falsifier for L1

Run a false-green challenge against the integrated measured revision: execute the frozen gate with the final reviewed allowlist and require zero unallowlisted hits, zero hits in all three `must_redact_paths`, and no allowlist rows for those paths; separately run its required synthetic failure arms (including a forbidden allowlist and multi-class fixture), and have a reviewer perform an independent whole-tree reading sweep for the known source-derived material **and paraphrased variants**, auditing every allowlist row. The contract separately requires the gate to reproduce the frozen base population hit-for-hit and exit 1 at d005cdd and on planted fixtures (`contract.md:24-29,55-61`).

**C1 fails** if the measured revision has any unallowlisted hit, any hit or allowlist on a required-redaction path, any unjustified allowlist entry, any synthetic planted finding the gate fails to catch, or any reviewer-confirmed private-source value still present in author-controlled tracked content—even if the regex total says zero. The documented non-author/history residuals do not fail the *narrow* snapshot metric when correctly excluded and routed; they do fail a claim of complete privacy remediation.

Keep 116→0 explicitly labeled as an **unallowlisted regex class-hit count**; report the 37/40 routed line distinction, review every allowlist, and require the independent whole-tree paraphrase sweep.
