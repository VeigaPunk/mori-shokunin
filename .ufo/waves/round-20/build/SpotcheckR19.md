## 3. Quality criteria to add or amend in round 20

These are drafts for the round-20 lesson. Each should cite the round-20 lesson that produces it.

- **Q64 (new).** A missing report may carry a cause (provider, cancelled, dead, killed, failed) only with a committed record that names that seat and its error. Aggregate counts, unnamed error-class lists and registry rows without status never establish a cause; otherwise the cause is `unknown`. Falsifier: a settlement row or summary assigns a cause without such a record. Evidence: R11.
- **Q65 (new).** A minimized or sanitized copy never moves a pinned digest. The mapping records the entering-revision digest, the present digest, and whether the artifact was bound. A bound artifact is not rewritten at HEAD; a rerun uses its original revision. Falsifier: a rerun or pin cites a present digest as historical, or a bound artifact changes without a mapping row. Evidence: R03, R04.
- **Q66 (new; rider on the C2 fix).** A coverage or settlement claim is complete only over the dispatch records it can prove. Zero discovered records, or unreadable or ignored dispatch-shaped JSON for a registered wave, is non-pass. Evidence: S02.
- **Q56 amendment.** Name the digest serialization: sorted keys, compact separators, UTF-8 without ASCII escaping, over `{context, tasks}`, no trailing newline (mori-dispatch-ledger.py:153–157). A row or receipt using any other serialization is unbound. A ledger is an array of attempt rows carrying `at`, a canonical `payload_sha256`, and `result` in refused/started/partial; any other top-level shape is failed. Evidence: R05, S01.
