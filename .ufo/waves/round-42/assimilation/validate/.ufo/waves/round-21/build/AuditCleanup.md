## Finding — High: the contract does not make same-identity receipt authorship a fail-closed disposition check

**Concrete counterexample (contract-level; not an observed current disposition).** The real deletion commit `e20f4fa3fd3fc9550641af38da3eddcf3d35f528` and post-hoc receipt commit `2f303dc6e30d7650e1e513e3cbad75412efdc353` both have author and committer `souji-shokunin <souji@shokunin.local>`. They are dated 2026-10-18 23:58:39 and 2026-10-19 00:02:43 respectively (read-only `git show -s --format='%H%n%aN <%aE>%n%cN <%cE>%n%aI%n%cI'` against the respective source repositories). The receipt rows say `pushed`; Souji's documented meaning is recoverability/removable-after-quarantine, not lifecycle disposition (`.ufo/waves/round-21/build/ConnectSouji.md:21-29,36-38,61-67`).

The frozen rule nevertheless permits `state: disposed` from a hash-bound `independent_disposition` with `independent:true`, a distinct actor, wave ID, and `settlement|cleanup-accounted` (`contract.md:14`); it defines the provenance shape as path/revision/hash but does not specify an independently verifiable source for either actor identity or disposition authorship. The active province explicitly says actor identity fields are declared evidence, not authenticated by the hash checker (`PROVINCE.md:177-181`). **[INFERENCE]** A cleanup actor can therefore copy/reference the same-identity `pushed` receipt in a new hash-bound per-wave record, assert `independent:true` and a different actor string plus `cleanup-accounted`, and meet the stated checks while the actual author remains the cleanup identity. Hash validation preserves this self-assertion; it does not establish independent authorship. That would convert lost required evidence to `disposed` and permit the pin, contrary to the requirement.

Current evidence does **not** exercise this path: both loss-accounting rows remain `unresolved` with `independent_disposition:null` and Souji as cleanup actor (`corpus/evidence/loss-accounting-r21.json:17-23,38-45`); historical provenance records null disposition and unknown settlement (`.ufo/waves/round-21/evidence/historical-provenance.json:19-40`).

**Smallest safe change:** tighten `contract.md:14` to say a same-identity `pushed`/cleanup receipt can never itself satisfy `independent_disposition` or cause a row to become `disposed`; require a separate per-wave disposition record whose author/actor is independently adjudicated against the cleanup identity, not taken from a self-declared field in that record. If that independent authorship check is absent or unknown, retain `unresolved` and `pin_allowed:false`. Keep `classify`'s disposition input derived from the validated audit result for operational use; its current boolean-only case schema (`contract.md:15`) is suitable only as synthetic rule-test input.
