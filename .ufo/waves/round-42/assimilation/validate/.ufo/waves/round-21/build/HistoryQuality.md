## C1 — freshness: retained loss evidence and acceptance gate

- **Q64 — partial; relevant counting/absence warning, but its wave-settlement scanner is not itself C1's evaluator.** The falsifier found a successful silent-zero scan through a symlinked wave root; dispatch-shaped files could be ignored or unreadable despite exit 0; `empty` was omitted from status counts; `missing` disagreed with its per-wave list; counts did not compare settlement state; receipt time/snapshot could be stale or absent; and discovery was limited in root/schema (`ReviewQuality.md:58-73`). The C1 connection is methodological: a before/after count cannot certify “0 omitted” if its evaluator can silently return zero, omit identities/states, or use a stale snapshot. C1 therefore calls for one evaluator over committed snapshots, with the required identity set and accounting states explicitly represented (`round-21/contract.md:7,13-16`). Q64's separate per-seat marker, schema-v2, misroot and terminal-cause defects are not evidence that the new C1 evaluator has those same defects; they remain routed debt unless directly reused.

- **Q65 — broken; directly load-bearing for fail-closed counting and the operational gate.** The falsifier reports invalid registers scoring zero under Q65's named counting command, a mismatch between that command and the strict checker's closed-set findings, locale-dependent results (`en_US.utf8` versus `C`), an evidence pointer checked only for non-blank text, and a re-pin branch with no `census-regression` precondition (`ReviewQuality.md:41-54`, especially 47-53). These failures map directly to C1's requirements: path/revision/SHA-256 provenance must be validated rather than merely present, and unresolved accounting must block acceptance in the actual audit outcome, not only in prose. The round-21 interface specifies committed evidence copies and an unresolved-state pin block (`round-21/contract.md:13-16`). This is a required design response, not proof that its implementation passes those checks.

- **Q67 — broken; its identity loss and cleanup/live-state conflation explain why C1 needs retained per-wave rows.** The falsifier found that Q67 permits a re-pin without a non-author per-wave record, conflates the “empty-by-removal” duty in Q24 with `live-state` plus `blind_spot`, leaves “unchanged census code” and “still-real condition” undefined, omits row identity, and leaves 17-versus-18 removed-file counts unreconciled (`ReviewQuality.md:24-38`). Its row-identity gap is directly pertinent to retaining `aggregator-r10` and `aggregator-r10-resume` separately. Its cleanup finding is also pertinent: a cleanup receipt is not independent lifecycle disposition. The current round-21 contract expressly requires a distinct independent disposition for a disposed row and keeps unresolved rows blocking (`:14-16,27`). The observed accounting file has both named rows `unresolved` with null independent disposition (`corpus/evidence/loss-accounting-r21.json:5-6,23,26-27,44`); this is a read-only observation, not verification of its provenance or the audit's enforcement.

- **Q68 — partial; relevant digest-integrity warning, while most of its rerun/mapping scope is routed debt.** The falsifier records the R03 allegation that working copies differed from historical digests and warns that a pin/rerun could cite a present digest as historical; it also records a dropped mapping-row falsifier and undefined “bound”/“mapping” terms. The relevant C1 principle is to bind each provenance pointer to a committed path, revision and SHA-256, and reject stale/mismatched evidence; the contract specifies that shape (`round-21/contract.md:14`). The broader minimized-copy rerun policy, R03 recheck and Q68 wording defects are not independently established as necessary to C1/C2 and remain qualified debt.

- **Q66 — partial; disclosed, not a C1/C2 repair target.** The falsifier found the start-marker detector recognizes only exact bytes, so whitespace/case/CRLF variants are incorrectly counted as delivered reports (`ReviewQuality.md:75-82`). C1 is an evidence-loss accounting and acceptance gate, not per-seat report settlement; C2 is classification of required evidence loss, not marker parsing. Unless either round-21 evaluator reuses this marker counter, this defect does not change the two claims and should remain routed debt, not expand scope into `mori-wave-settlement.py`.
