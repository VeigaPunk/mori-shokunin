## One bounded proposal for Mori

Within the already-frozen C1 tool/selftest contract, add a synthetic fixture that drives a matching hit through the shared JSON/diagnostic renderer and verifies both sides of the boundary: the fixture yields the expected stable finding and exit 1, while the synthetic matched text is absent from **stdout and stderr**. If an assertion fails, print only a fixed failure id / stream label; do not dump captured raw output. Keep the scan’s frozen whole-tree scope and metadata-only report shape (`contract.md:47-60,52-53`); do not alter `c1-policy.json`, its metric, the freeze register, or the C2/C3 ledgers.

This improves C1’s demonstrable privacy boundary and C3-adjacent failure operability without changing calibration inputs or dispatch evidence. Runtime consequence is limited to exercising the existing Git-blob reader on the isolated selftest fixture under the contract’s caller-provided scratch directory; no live host/session data is needed. It follows Chousa’s ordering and the useful part of Kama’s two-stream assertion while avoiding Kama’s earlier raw-excerpt construction. Risk remains: a canary proves the renderer path, not regex recall or absence of private context outside frozen classes. Keep the policy’s reading-sweep limitation explicit and do not turn a zero pattern count into a claim about already-pushed history or unauthorized reads.
