## 4. Findings (bounded; R17 not falsifiable as written)

### F1 — LOW (inherited requirement-strength debt, not a cutover defect): R17 pins only exit code, finding id and empty stderr

R17's predicate cannot distinguish a *correct* malformed-receipt rejection from a *degraded* one.
Concrete counterexample that **passes R17 verbatim** while being wrong:

```json
{"schema":"mori-retention-reconciliation-v2","finding_id":"RETENTION_RECONCILIATION_SETUP",
 "error":"receipt_schema","privacy_clearance":true,"detail":"R22_SAMPLE retained-red"}
```
printed on stdout with exit 2 and empty stderr satisfies `record('R17','malformed',2,'RETENTION_RECONCILIATION_SETUP')`.

This is not a hypothetical: r22's own mutation battery measured it. In
`.ufo/waves/round-22/evidence/measure3/summary.json` → `mutations.cases`:

* `setup-leak` (mutant injected `"detail":"R22_SAMPLE retained-red"` into the setup envelope):
  **`frozen_unmet: 0`** — all 24 frozen requirements passed, R17 among them; the mutant was killed
  only by `refine:*` rows and `safety:all-arm-output`.
* `false-clearance` (mutant set `privacy_clearance: true`): R17 still `True`; killed by R02/R11/R23
  plus refinements.

So R17 (and the frozen 24 as a class) is blind to: the **error reason** (`invalid_json` vs any other
setup token), the **setup envelope's key set**, **non-leakage of retained content**, and
**`privacy_clearance` false** on the malformed arm. R23 does not re-run that battery —
`evidence/measure4/summary.json.scope_notes.r22_reproduction` states it verbatim: *"no historical r22
mutant battery is adapted here"*. For this cutover the exposure is closed by other means, and I
verified those means:

* the byte guard (A4) makes any behaviour-changing mutation of the pinned file impossible at
  `f3dc07c` — a mutant cannot pass the guard, so it cannot be the measured artifact; and
* the same-instance replay (C23) compares the setup-shape envelope key-by-key before vs after on the
  identical fixture instance, so a cutover-induced envelope change would be caught.

**Disposition: no edit.** Strengthening R17 would silently redefine a frozen original requirement and
break the pre-registered original-26 denominator (the amendment forbids redefining that denominator).
Recorded here as residual risk for the parent, and as the reason the r23 C1 claim must keep the byte
guard in its claim set — the guard, not R17, is what carries "no other behaviour changed".

are not re-run in r23. The production code path is nonetheless unchanged by the cutover (A4), and
`parse()` raises `Invalid('invalid_json')` there identically, i.e. the un-covered path behaves
correctly — the gap is in *evidence coverage*, not in behaviour.

## 6. Residual risk and verification gaps

1. **R17 cannot, by itself, evidence the quality of the malformed-arm rejection** (F1). Any claim
   that "C1 changed nothing else" must rest on C21 (byte guard) + C23 (same-instance replay), not on
   the 26/26 count. The 26/26 figure must not be quoted as behavioural proof of unchanged
   semantics; it is proof that the frozen predicates still hold on the frozen fixtures.
2. **The r22 refinement battery was not re-run in r23** (`scope_notes.r22_reproduction`), and it is
   the only component that historically detected setup-envelope leakage and clearance lies. The
   argument that this is safe here is the byte guard plus the identity of the entering blob with r22's
   validated tool (`0795bac9…`) — I verified both; I did not re-run any mutant.
3. **Inner-receipt malformation is unmeasured in r23** (F2); it is unmeasured *and* unmeasurable
   without a new fixture variant, which the freeze forbids adding to the original-26 denominator.
4. **Byte-guard scope is one file.** `tools/mori-retention-reconcile.py` is guarded; any other file
   that could influence the tool's observable behaviour (docs/SKILL prose, wrapper scripts) is not
   covered by the guard — F3 shows the prose is consistent, but consistency there is not guard-enforced.
5. **No live-host reads** were performed by this seat (bounds), so nothing here bears on the C1
   real control's host observation; C25/C35 were verified as *driver checks*, and the frozen real
   control bytes were read, not re-measured.
