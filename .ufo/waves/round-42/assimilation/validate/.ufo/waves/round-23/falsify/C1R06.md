This is *not* a defect: the oracle is self-consistent (`R24` asserts `input_revision == f['rev']`) and the replay (Axis D) reuses one instance, so no cross-root revision equality is ever required.

## 10. Residual risks and verification gaps (bounded)

1. **R06 asserts the verdict, not the reason.** The predicate checks exit code, finding id and empty stderr; it does not assert `differences == ['hits']`. A mutant that reached `MISMATCH` for an unrelated reason inside the inject arm would satisfy R06 as written. Mitigated — but not by R06 — by the recorded `differences ['hits']`, by the byte guard (C21/C22) and by the same-instance replay (C23). *Recommendation for a future wave only: a predicate asserting `differences == ['hits']`. Frozen bytes must not change now.*
2. **R06 does not pin the direction of the deviation.** R05 (`drop`) and R06 (`inject`) yield the same predicate shape and the same `differences` label `['hits']`; neither asserts "extra" vs "missing". The pair does not separate the two directions; only the suite as a whole does (R08).
3. **Granularity limits outside R06's arm:** multiplicity is R19 (duplicate ⇒ setup, exit 2); count-vs-set equality is R08; alias/schema state is R25. R06 covers exactly "an extra aggregate hit in the receipt is detected".
4. **Verification gap — static only.** I executed nothing. The before/after observations cited are read from the committed measure4 receipts at `f3dc07c0`; their internal consistency is supported by the driver's own C08/C09 (sealed artefacts byte-identical seal→revision), C11 (dependency digests pinned), C12/C13 (working copies equal committed seal bytes) and the `tool_sha256`/`argv` pins inside each receipt. An independent re-run of the pinned driver was not authorized for this seat and was not attempted.
5. **Out of scope, recorded for completeness:** the C2 reds (`C34.c2_after_every_case_matches: false`, `C30.c2_oracle_real_control_agrees: false`) belong to the frozen `CaseEscapedSpace` contradiction and bear on no C1 requirement, including R06.
