## Findings by severity

No defect-severity findings. Informational notes:

- **[LOW] Receipt records the literal `revision` argument, not the resolved SHA** (`run-r41-controls.py` header writes `args.revision` verbatim). A short or symbolic arg would leave the measured revision ambiguous in the receipt. `checkout --detach` still fails closed on an unresolvable rev, and `head_pin` would pin whatever was actually checked out — but the receipt itself wouldn't name it. Both actual receipts pass full 40-hex, so no impact here.
- **[LOW] `head_pin` evidence rides the C1 sections only.** C2/C3 receipt sections do not independently echo the sandbox HEAD; their binding to the measured revision rests on the runner's detached-checkout step plus `SystemExit`. Acceptable; a one-line `git -C clone rev-parse HEAD` in the header would make the receipt self-contained for all sections.
- **[INFO] Before-mode instrument provenance is working-tree, not commit-pinned**: the before run copies fixture files from the lane working tree into the sandbox (`copy_tree`/`copy_file` from `source`). `freeze-verify.txt` retroactively pinned those bytes at `d72ac167` with 0 mismatches. Outside this review's after-evidence scope; recorded for completeness.
