## Proposed move

Add one concise output-boundary caveat beside the Round22 instructions in `/home/vgpnk/Projects/shokunin/mori-shokunin/SKILL.md`: `line_sha256` is unsalted provenance and can be guessable for low-entropy lines; it is not redaction, secrecy, authentication, privacy clearance, or pushed-history exposure authority. Note that the command echoes its own `argv`, so invocation values must not contain secrets. This documentation-only clarification preserves exact identities and all frozen requirement meanings; no adverse effect on coverage, operability, depth, freshness, or adjacency is identified.
