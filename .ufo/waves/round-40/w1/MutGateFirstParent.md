## Verdict

**KILLED** — the mutant dies exactly on the dag-p arm and nowhere else
(15→14 pass, 1 fail). The detector (the gate's own dag-p expectation) is
sensitive to the per-parent fall-through primitive, and the P-C11 clause it
probes is exercised by a committed fixture. The claim's kill rule "a gate with
the second-parent fall-through fabricates the merge-fixture edit → the frozen
fixture fails" is confirmed by direct execution, not by inspection.

Coverage note for the main agent (not a defect in the claim): the printed
dag-p `changed S001 …` line does not distinguish the two byte states; any
future receipt that cites only that line as the before/after would be
insensitive. The distinguishing evidence is the `supersede_timing` line and
the selftest arm.
