## What the v7.2 → v7.3 diff changes, and what a careless reader would miss

Counts recomputed with the ledger's own formula
`diff -u <v7.2 copy> <v7.3 copy> | grep -c '^[-+][^-+]'`: proc.rst 3, mm_inline.h 49,
show_mem.c 22, meminfo.c 0 — all four match the ledger's summary table exactly.

- **proc.rst (3 changed lines, none in the meminfo section).** (a) line 516, a typo fix in the
  PSS/smaps prose: "any page page of the larger allocation" → "any page of the larger
  allocation"; (b) line 611, a **new** VmFlags entry inserted into the VmFlags table:
  `ur  userfaultfd read-write-protect tracking`. That insertion shifts every line after it by
  +1, which is exactly why rows 1–5 sit one line lower in v7.3 than in v7.2 while their text is
  byte-identical. Anyone who compares line numbers instead of text will wrongly conclude the
  meminfo definitions moved; they did not. (Note for the record: the peer seat Mo53Fr02's
  report cites the v7.3 line numbers for both copies in rows 1–5 — e.g. "v7.2 proc.rst quote:
  line 1129; v7.3: line 1129" — which does not show the +1 shift. The claim contents are
  unaffected; only the line citations differ, and mine are the measured ones.)
- **fs/proc/meminfo.c: byte-identical** (same sha256 both tags). Nothing can have changed here;
  rows 6, 7 and 8 are carried over verbatim. The S504 SOURCES row's "byte-identical to v7.2"
  wording is confirmed by an independent re-fetch, not only by the lane's own copy.
- **include/linux/mm_inline.h (49 changed lines).** All 49 are outside `folio_is_file_lru`: the
  removal of the uffd-wp pte helper `pte_install_uffd_wp_if_needed()` (59 lines) and the
  `memdesc_section(cur_page->flags)` → `memdesc_section(&cur_page->flags)` change in
  `num_pages_contiguous()` (v7.3 lines 600 and 610). The file shrank by 1750 bytes, and
  `folio_is_file_lru` stayed at line 28 only because every edit is below it. A reader who sees
  "49 changed lines in mm_inline.h" and assumes the LRU classification was reworked would be
  wrong.
- **mm/show_mem.c (22 changed lines).** All outside `si_mem_available()`: a new
  `#include "page_alloc.h"` (which is what shifts the function down by exactly one line),
  `nodemask_t *` → `const nodemask_t *` on `show_mem_node_skip()` / `show_free_areas()` /
  `__show_mem()`, printk unit fixes (`%luKB` → `%lukB`, `%ukB` → `%lukB` for `local_pcp`),
  `%ld` → `%lu` for the "total pagecache pages" line, and an `(unsigned long)` cast on
  `this_cpu_read()`. The `si_mem_available()` body itself is byte-identical between the tags.
  A reader could mistake the `%ld`/`%lu` printk churn (including the `%lu`→`%ld` flip on the
  hwpoisoned line) for a change in how memory is accounted; it is kernel-log debug output in
  `show_mem()` and has no effect on the estimate or on `/proc/meminfo`. Likewise, `show_mem.c`
  feeds a `printk` dump, while `/proc/meminfo` is produced by `fs/proc/meminfo.c` calling
  `si_mem_available()` — the two paths are independent, so show_mem.c edits do not reach the
  meminfo lines.
- **Cross-file rule check.** Rows 9 and 10 name behaviour that the comment states but the code
  implements elsewhere. Fetched and diffed at both tags: `include/linux/page-flags.h`
  (PG_swapbacked comment identical), `mm/madvise.c` (MADV_FREE branches byte-equivalent; the 42
  changed lines are shifts from an unrelated addition), `mm/shmem.c` (the three
  `__folio_set_swapbacked()` sites present in both tags). The end-to-end rule therefore survives
  v7.3, not merely the quote.
