so a burst of kills can leave Killed process lines without them. v7.3 `oom_kill_process`
(1008–1054): `if (__ratelimit(&oom_rs)) { dump_header(oc); dump_oom_victim(oc, victim); }` at

victim line bare. Shared static `oom_rs` (1012–1013) applies to global and memcg kills alike;
`oom_kill_memcg_member` kills also produce headerless victim lines. Holds.
Careless-reader note: the asymmetry runs both ways — `out_of_memory` calls `dump_header(oc)`
unconditionally at 1158 in the no-killable-process path, so headers without a Killed line also
occur (different mechanism than the claim, same log ambiguity).
