## Findings

**Finding 1 — minor. F10's [INFERENCE] misattributes two numbers to "those records".**
The entry says: "The number of refused seats in one wave (64, 66, 68, 88 in those records) exceeds 100 in some waves…". Checked:
- The two cited records contain refusals of 64 (GtLaneKasegi.md:48) and 66/72/86 (GtLaneKama.md:72-75). `grep -o '\b68\b|\b88\b'` over both files: zero hits.
- 68 *is* a refusal count in other round-20/build records: GtLaneChousa.md:61 ("refused all 68 … refused 68") and GtLaneKaidoku.md:86 ("all 68 refused").
- 88 appears in round-20/build only as a dispatch count, never a refusal: GtLaneKumitate.md:72 `dispatch_count` 88; TrailerVariants.md:81 "88 tasks, all match".
- No single-wave refusal count in those records exceeds 100 (largest 86), so "exceeds 100 in some waves" is not supported by them as written.
The mechanism the sentence exists to carry (each seat re-registers on every dispatch attempt, every attempt fails once the session count is at the cap, the cap is session-wide, not a per-wave budget) is sound and supported — Kasegi alone records four consecutive refused attempts of 64 names each.
*Smallest fix:* rewrite the parenthetical to cite what the records show, e.g. "64 (Kasegi:48), 66/72/86 (Kama:72-75), 68 (Chousa:61, Kaidoku:86) — and drop 88 (a dispatch count, Kumitate:72)"; replace "exceeds 100 in some waves" with the supported arithmetic (e.g. "one lane's four refused attempts alone are 256 refused registrations against a 100 cap"). Touches only that [INFERENCE] sentence; no claim value changes.
